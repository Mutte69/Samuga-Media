Corrections policy

Effective date: 26 July 2026

Readers can report a possible error through our contact channels. We review the article, source material and publication history.

Corrections

Material errors are corrected as soon as reasonably possible. Significant corrections may be noted inside the article with the date and a clear explanation of what changed.

Updates

Developing stories may be updated as new information becomes available. An updated timestamp does not necessarily mean an earlier version was wrong.

Removal requests

Published material is not removed merely because it is inconvenient. We may correct, limit, archive or remove content where there is a compelling legal, safety, privacy or accuracy reason.
