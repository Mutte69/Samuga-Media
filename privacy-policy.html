Privacy policy

Effective date: 26 July 2026

Samuga Media collects only the information reasonably needed to operate the website, respond to messages, protect the service and understand general usage.

Information you provide

When you send a tip, contact us or use a newsroom account, we may receive the information you submit, account details and related communication records.

Technical information

Our hosting and security providers may process IP addresses, device information, request logs and cookies needed for security and delivery. The site also loads content from our API and may use third-party font or media delivery services. Samuga Media records basic first-party pageview statistics using a randomly generated browser session identifier that is irreversibly hashed by our server; our newsroom analytics table does not store readers’ raw IP addresses.

How information is used

Information may be used to deliver content, answer enquiries, manage newsroom access, prevent abuse, diagnose failures and improve the service.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be handled by service providers needed for hosting, storage, communications, analytics or security, or disclosed where legally required.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the contact page.
