About Samuga Media

Samuga Media is an independent Maldivian digital newsroom focused on making important information clear, fast and easy to understand. We publish in English and Dhivehi across our website, Telegram community and social platforms.

Our purpose

We aim to reduce confusion, explain what matters and help readers follow developing stories without unnecessary complexity. Technology and AI may assist our newsroom, but responsibility for what we publish remains with Samuga Media.

Contact the newsroom

News tips, corrections and business enquiries can be sent through our contact page or Telegram channels.
